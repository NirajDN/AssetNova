Optimum Inventory Control of Machine Spares and Co
==================================================

CORE FUNCTIONALITIES:
---------------------
1. Dashboard Overview
   - View high-level metrics (Total Parts, Out of Stock, Low Stock Alerts, Total Asset Value).
   - Recent activity feed and quick action shortcuts.

2. Parts Directory (Inventory Management)
   - Add, Edit, View, and Delete machine spares and components.
   - Track part number, name, category, warehouse location, cost, and quantities (current stock, min required threshold).
   
3. Stock Transactions (In / Out)
   - Record inward stock (new purchases, returns).
   - Record outward stock (parts used, dispatched for maintenance).
   - Maintain a comprehensive history/log for auditing.

4. Global Suppliers
   - Manage supplier profiles (Name, Contact, Address, Rating).
   - Link parts to specific suppliers for easy reordering.

5. Maintenance Schedule
   - Track upcoming machine maintenance.
   - Detail which spare parts are projected to be needed.
   
6. Warehouse Mapping
   - Organize and visualize where parts are stored physically (Aisles, Racks, Bins).

7. Analytics Vault
   - Detailed reporting, historical data charting, and optimization insights (like EOQ - Economic Order Quantity).

8. System Settings & Authentication
   - Login, registration, role-based access control (Admin vs. Operator).

TECHNICAL STACK & ARCHITECTURE:
-------------------------------
- Backend: PHP Laravel MVC framework
- Frontend: Blade Templates + UI extracted from Stitch (HTML/CSS)
- DB: MySQL or SQLite
